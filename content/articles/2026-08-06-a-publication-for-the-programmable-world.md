A language does not become a world merely because its compiler works. It becomes a world when people can see what others are making, argue about its direction, teach one another, and carry its ideas into places the core maintainers never planned.

Hara World is the publication layer for that work.

It begins with a deliberately ordinary primitive: an article with a stable URL. The article may be an original dispatch, an official release, a field note, or an excerpt syndicated from an independent publication. It records authorship, provenance, dates, topics, licensing, and corrections. That record is more important than any individual social network.

## One source, many transmissions

Once an article is approved, Hara World can derive the rest:

- an RSS and JSON Feed entry;
- a newsletter edition or digest block;
- short copy adapted to each social network;
- a narration script and timed captions;
- a vertical storyboard and review video;
- a longer demonstration brief for a human presenter.

Those outputs are projections of the canonical article. They do not become separate, untraceable versions of the truth.

## A syndicate, not a scraper

Writers should be able to submit their own RSS or Atom feeds. Hara World then follows a public source registry that states what may be carried: a link, an excerpt, or full text under an explicit licence or permission.

The default is an excerpt with attribution and a canonical link. A feed being technically readable is not the same as permission to republish it.

Trusted sources can be checked automatically, but new entries arrive as reviewable changes. Merge is the editorial decision. This allows the publication to automate repetition without automating judgement.

## Propagation without deception

The word *propaganda* is useful when it is stripped of euphemism: material designed to propagate an idea. Hara World should be persuasive, ambitious, and recognisable. It should also make its methods visible.

Generated summaries are labelled. Synthetic narration is disclosed. Community writing is not presented as an official announcement. Claims link to evidence. Corrections stay attached to the canonical record.

The goal is not to fill every feed every day. The goal is to make Hara’s strongest ideas legible, memorable, and easy to follow—and to give independent builders a place in the public story.

This is the first transmission.
